Research Thesis Project

"Evaluation of Morpho-Physiological Traits and Yield Stability in Wheat (Triticum aestivum) Under Variable Coastal Salinity Gradients."

Supervisor: Dr. Kawsar Hossain, Assistant Professor, Department of Agriculture, NSTU.

Soil salinity in coastal Bangladesh poses severe challenges to rabi cereal production. This field investigation utilizes a Randomized Complete Block Design (RCBD) across multiple salinity regimes (0, 4, 8, 12 dS/m) to quantify vegetative endurance, tiller survival, flag leaf photosynthesis duration, and harvest index in Triticum aestivum genotypes. The objective is to identify physiological indicators associated with sustained grain filling and osmotic tolerance.
